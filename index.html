Mathematics (Relations & Functions + Sets)

Q1. Let $A = \{1, 2, 3\}$. Define a relation $R$ on $A$ as $R = \{(1,1), (2,2), (3,3), (1,2), (2,1)\}$. Determine if $R$ is an equivalence relation. (CBSE/CUET)

Step 1: Reflexivity
For every $a \in A$, $(a,a) \in R$. Here, $(1,1), (2,2), (3,3) \in R$. Hence, $R$ is reflexive.

Step 2: Symmetry
Since $(1,2) \in R$ and its reverse $(2,1) \in R$, the relation is symmetric.

Step 3: Transitivity
Check combinations: $(1,2) \in R$ and $(2,1) \in R \implies (1,1) \in R$. This holds true for all elements. Hence, $R$ is transitive.

Conclusion: Since $R$ is reflexive, symmetric, and transitive, it is an equivalence relation.

Physics (Electric Charges and Fields)

Q1. Two point charges $q_1 = +2\mu\text{C}$ and $q_2 = -6\mu\text{C}$ are placed at a distance of $10\text{ cm}$ in a vacuum. Calculate the electrostatic force between them. (CBSE/NDA)

Formula: $F = \frac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_0} \cdot \frac{|q_1 \cdot q_2|}{r^2}$

Given values:
$q_1 = 2 \times 10^{-6}\text{ C}$, $q_2 = 6 \times 10^{-6}\text{ C}$
$r = 0.1\text{ m} \implies r^2 = 0.01\text{ m}^2$
$\frac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_0} = 9 \times 10^9\text{ N}\cdot\text{m}^2/\text{C}^2$

Calculation:
$F = \frac{9 \times 10^9 \times (2 \times 10^{-6} \times 6 \times 10^{-6})}{0.01}$
$F = \frac{9 \times 10^9 \times 12 \times 10^{-12}}{10^{-2}} = \frac{108 \times 10^{-3}}{10^{-2}} = 10.8\text{ N}$

Conclusion: The force is $10.8\text{ N}$ and it is attractive in nature due to opposite charges.

Chemistry (Solutions)

Q1. Calculate the molarity of a solution containing $5\text{g}$ of $\text{NaOH}$ dissolved in $450\text{mL}$ of solution. (CBSE/CUET)

Step 1: Calculate moles of $\text{NaOH}$
$\text{Molar mass of NaOH} = 23 + 16 + 1 = 40\text{ g/mol}$
$\text{Moles of NaOH} = \frac{\text{Given mass}}{\text{Molar mass}} = \frac{5}{40} = 0.125\text{ moles}$

Step 2: Convert volume to Liters
$\text{Volume} = \frac{450}{1000} = 0.45\text{ L}$

Step 3: Calculate Molarity (M)
$M = \frac{\text{Moles of solute}}{\text{Volume of solution in L}} = \frac{0.125}{0.45} \approx 0.278\text{ M}$

Answer: $0.278\text{ mol/L}$
